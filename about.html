About Us

The Garden Collective is a horticultural society dedicated to bringing people together through a shared love of plants, gardening, and nature.

We provide a welcoming space where members can learn, exchange ideas, and develop their gardening skills. From beginners to experienced gardeners, everyone is encouraged to participate and share their knowledge.

Our Mission

To cultivate knowledge, community, and a deeper appreciation for the natural world through horticulture.
